landcover_assignment.national_landcover

National areas and soil, peat and burnt shares for each land use, read from the package database (via Loader).

Each land use’s calibration-year row is its area plus 17 share columns. Every share is one database column divided by the land use’s total area (total_kha), applied to the land uses in SHARE_SOURCES and 0 for the rest.

Forest is different (decision D6a): it mirrors cbm_runner 0.6.1, whose historic forest is aligned with the NID and forest expert judgement (package database managed_forest_data and forest_fire_rate, derived from cbm_runner’s disturbance events):

  • area = cbm_runner managed forest, FM (present before 1990) + AF (planted since 1990), in that year;

  • share_organic / share_organic_mineral = the NID 2026 drained N-poor / N-rich hectares of the calibration year (Tables 6.7 + 6.15, decision D3) / area. The open area and roads that CBM doesn’t model are not emitting (as in the NID), and AF planting after the calibration year is on mineral soil, so the drained hectares stay fixed and share_mineral is the rest;

  • share_burnt = cbm_runner FM + AF fire in that year / area.

Target-year forest rows (NationalLandCover.get_forest_target_row()) add the scenario’s afforestation to the area, and cbm_runner’s scenario fire on it (rate x afforestation) to the burnt area.

The grassland area is not a database area: it is grassland_production’s herd-derived area plus the national balance (see NationalLandCover.get_grassland_balance_area() and docs/grassland_area.md).

get_forest_target_row(calibration_year, target_year, scenario_afforestation)

The national forest row in target_year for a scenario that afforests scenario_afforestation ha (decision D6a, mirroring cbm_runner 0.6.1). With target_year == calibration_year and no afforestation it is the calibration-year row.

  • area_ha = managed forest (FM + AF) in the target year + scenario afforestation;

  • drained N-poor / N-rich hectares = the NID calibration-year hectares (fixed; all new forest is mineral);

  • burnt ha = FM + AF fire in the target year + scenario fire rate x scenario afforestation (cbm_runner plants the scenario’s afforestation by its afforestation end year and burns rate x the area planted so far).

Returns:

dict with area_ha and every share in ROW_COLUMNS (0 for shares that don’t apply to forest).

get_grassland_balance_area(year, total_grassland_area)

National grassland that the herd doesn’t need: the national grassland area minus the herd-derived grassland area, in year (the calibration year). It is added to the grassland row so that its emissions are counted, it stays the same in every scenario, and it is never available to be spared. National tree only.

Sources:

  • national grassland area: package database, grassland_data.total_kha (EPA CRT 2026 Table4.C, total grassland, 1990-2024), via get_national_area();

  • herd-derived grassland area: grassland_production’s total_grassland input, calibration-year row, via get_derived_national_grassland_area().

Parameters:

  • year (int) – The calibration year.

  • total_grassland_area (pandas.DataFrame) – The total_grassland input (grassland_production).

Returns:

The balance (ha). If the herd area exceeds the national area, 0, with a warning.

Return type:

float

Raises:

ValueError – If year is not in the database.
